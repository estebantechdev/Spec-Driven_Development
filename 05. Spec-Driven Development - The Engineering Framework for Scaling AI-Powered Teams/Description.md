The provided source details Spec-Driven Development (SDD), a methodology that uses Artificial Intelligence to transform software engineering into a process guided by formal, exhaustive specifications. Instead of focusing on manual coding, developers act as architects and validators who define technical requirements and business logic as the primary "source of truth." This approach leverages frameworks like Open Spec and tools such as Cursor or Claude to automate implementation while preventing the accumulation of technical debt. By utilizing standardized prompts and structured workflows, teams can ensure consistent quality, comprehensive testing, and updated documentation across complex codebases. Ultimately, the text argues that the developer’s role is shifting from writing lines of code to high-level design and verification, significantly increasing productivity.
